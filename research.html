Research Interests

My research interests lie in neutrino astronomy and astrophysics, with a particular focus on neutrino source searches and Physics beyond the Standard Model (BSM), including Dark Matter. I am currently involved in astrophysical neutrino analyses with the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and software development and sensitivity studies for the Pacific Ocean Neutrino Experiment (P-ONE). I also have experience in detector hardware development, calibration, and simulations, and I am always happy to discuss multi-messenger astronomy and astrophysics!

Experiences

P-ONE Collaboration

  • Development of a software pipeline for neutrino point-source searches (2025-present).
  • Point-source sensitivity studies for P-ONE (2025-present).

IceCube Collaboration

  • All-sky searches for astrophysical neutrino sources (2025-present).
  • The first dedicated experimental search for interactions between dark matter and high-energy astrophysical neutrinos (2022-present).
  • Simulation studies of camera-based detector calibration systems (2014-2022).
  • Development and mass testing of calibration hardware for the IceCube Upgrade (2014-2022).

COSINE-100 Collaboration

  • Detector simulations (2016-2017).
  • COSINE-100 detector construction (2015-2016).
