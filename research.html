Research Interests

In these days, I am interested in the Neutrino Astrophysics and Astronomy as well as the Beyond the Standard Model (BSM) physics including Dark Matter (DM) searches using the high-energy astrophysical neutrinos with the IceCube Neutrino Telescope. I also have experiences on the detector hardware and their calibrations, and I will be happy if I can discuss the multi-messenger Astrophysics and Astronomy.

Experiences

As a member of P-ONE Collaboration

  • Software pipeline development for neutrino point source searching analysis (2025-present).
  • Sensitivity studies for neutrino point source searching in P-ONE (2025-present).

As a member of IceCube Collaboration

  • Neutirno point source searching (2025-present).
  • BSM physics analysis searching for neutrino - dark matter interactions with point sources (2022-present).
  • Simulation studies for the camera-based detector calibration system (2014-2022).
  • Development and mass-testing of the calibration hardware for IceCube Upgrade (2014-2022).

As a member of COSINE-100 Collaboration

  • Detector simulations (2016-2017).
  • COSINE-100 detector construction (2015-2016).
